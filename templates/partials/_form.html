A form whose fields stand one to a row of a table names the template that draws that table (its `table_region`, each row's control drawn by partials/_row_field.html) as `table_body`: the form draws its hidden fields and lays out no fields of its own (no `.form`), so the table reaches its frame's sides as a table section's does, and its closing row sits under the table. `action` is where it posts, the page itself unless given; it takes a file when the form does. `verb`, `cancel_url`, `note`, `choices` and `choice_field` are the closing row's, and `bar` pins that row to the bottom of the screen once something has changed, for a long form. `next` is the page to come back to once it is saved. A form answered in place (`in_place`) is sent to the region it stands in and says how it went in its closing row, whose Cancel is answered there too. A form that asks rather than writes (a lookup) is `method="get"`: it carries no write token, since sending it twice changes nothing, and it never takes a file.
