{% comment %} The one card of the queue, on the dashboard, the queue page and a domain's own pages. Its face is `application.decisions`: what is wrong, what it means, since when if that is known, then what to do, where a step is a step, a reason is a sentence and anything that can be pressed is a button. Under a domain's own heading (`grouped`) a card does not repeat the domain. Where the reader sorts their own queue (`triage`) a card ends with the one thing they can do to it: dismiss it, or restore it. Neither fixes it. A dismissed card is its title. On the page of the thing a card is about (`here`) its title is plain text. A card drawn from a reading says how old it is (`_reading_age.html`). With `glance` (the dashboard) a card is its title, one line of what it means, its age and its buttons; its title opens the page that shows the rest (`page`). Its buttons and links sit at its end, beside its text, not on rows of their own. {% endcomment %}
