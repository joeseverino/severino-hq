Its face is `application.decisions`. The first line is the title and, only when urgent, the word after it; then its one line of meaning, then since when and, once out of date, how old its reading is, quiet, under it. What it offers is `{% row_actions %}`: the first two buttons, the rest waiting in the opened line, and where the reader sorts their own queue (`triage`), Dismiss or Restore as the last of them. Everything taller (the meaning in full, facts, advice, the steps and their commands, the reason it cannot be done from here, the pages that help) is `row-below`, opened by the row's toggle. Under a domain's own heading (`grouped`) a row does not repeat the domain; on the page of the thing it is about (`here`) its title is plain text; in a family (`folded`, the family row's id) it is folded under that row. A dismissed row is its title and the way back. In the dense form (`dense`) its meaning waits in the opened line too.
