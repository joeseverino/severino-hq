{% comment %} How urgent a card is, and behind it why: Cmd- or Ctrl-click opens what raised it (`partials/_origin_panel.html`, which `#panel` draws, by the caller after the line the pill sits in), and hovering says why in a tooltip. A plain click does nothing.
