{% comment %} One renderer for every registry-driven band on a detail page. A section is a heading, columns, rows of cells and an optional action, and nothing here knows what any of them mean. That is the point: a page grows a band because a resolver produced one, so adding what HQ knows next is a function and a registry entry rather than an edit to a template. Shared rather than copied. Two pages rendering the same dataclass from two templates drift the moment one gains a column, and the difference shows up as one page quietly missing what the other shows. A cell with a tone is a state, drawn as its status. What a row shows beneath itself (`below`, a run's evidence) is in its open line, drawn by {% run_evidence %}, and what may be done to it (`actions`) ends it, drawn by {% row_actions %}. A section whose rows are the latest of more links to the rest from its heading (`more_url`). Expects: `sections`: an iterable of ServiceSection; `spans_row`, True for the ones that take a flow's whole row, False for the short ones. A page draws the first ahead of its short sections and the second among them, so its flow paints in the order it reads (docs/Design.md, "Sibling sections flow"). {% endcomment %} {% for section in sections %}{% if section.spans_row == spans_row %} {% comment %} Folded, the heading is the toggle and its count says what is behind it.
