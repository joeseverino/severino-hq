An event that changed things heads them: its gutter holds the fold rather than the row's own toggle, so opening the fold opens what its columns that left hold too (tables.js). A fold carries at most history.SHOWN of what the event changed, a run at most as many moments; a last row (`rest`), one cell across the row, counts the others and links to the list of them all: an event's page, a run's shared page.
