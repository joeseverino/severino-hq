{% comment %} Tabbed charts — several drawings of one thing, in the space of one. {% include "partials/_chart_tabs.html" with charts=my_charts group="splits" %} `charts` is a sequence of LineChart or StackedBarChart, at most four. `group` must be unique on the page: it names the radio group the tabs are built from. Radios and CSS rather than JavaScript, for the same reason the rest of HQ is server-rendered: the panel works before any script runs, arrow keys move between tabs for free, and there is no state to fall out of step with the markup. The inputs are siblings of the panels rather than children of the tab strip, because the selector that shows a panel is a sibling combinator and cannot climb back out of a wrapper. The labels sit in the strip and point at them by id, which is what keeps the visual grouping without nesting the controls. {% endcomment %}
