{% comment %} A page's head, from `page` (application.pages.Page). The only place a head is drawn: a page template extends page.html rather than including this. `page_badges`, when a view supplies it, is what the thing is, beside its name: (label, tone, title) as application.pages.PageBadge. Context rather than a Page field, so the SDK's Page is unchanged. `page_aside`, the same way, names a template drawn on the title's line: what a page says about its subject at a glance (how it is doing, where it runs), beside its name rather than under it. `page_facts`, the same way, is markup: a line of short facts under the lede (where a thing lives and runs, when it last changed), drawn the head's width where the lede keeps the measure. The trail's line ends with the note of a page about one thing (`page_visit`, partials/_visit_refresh.html). `page_dateline`, the same way, is the day a page no trail leads to opens on (the dashboard's), drawn in the trail's place as a kicker. A head with both an aside and a line of facts is a column of its words (the trail, the title, the lede, the facts) with the aside beside the whole column, as the dashboard's readings stand beside its day, greeting and what needs you; with no facts line, the aside stays on the title's line. {% endcomment %}
