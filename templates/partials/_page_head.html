Context rather than a Page field, so the SDK's Page is unchanged. `page_aside`, the same way, names a template drawn on the title's line: what a page says about its subject at a glance (how it is doing, where it runs), beside its name rather than under it. `page_facts`, the same way, is markup: a line of short facts under the lede (where a thing lives and runs, when it last changed), drawn the head's width where the lede keeps the measure. {% endcomment %}
